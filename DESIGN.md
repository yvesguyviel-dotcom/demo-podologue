## Visual Theme
Sober, calm, and rigorous healthcare identity. High negative space, crisp structural lines, soft off-white background paired with deep medical sage and subtle muted slate accents.

## Art Direction
- Concept: Calm deontological healthcare folio and practice directory.
- Layout rhythm: Structured, breathable, asymmetrical editorial pacing with clean border divides.

## Typography
- Scale: editorial
- Headlines: Newsreader — elegant, restrained, authoritative medical serif
- Body: Plus Jakarta Sans — highly legible, neutral, accessible sans-serif

## Components
- Motif: Fine 1px border framing (`border-border`), squared or subtly rounded corners (`rounded-none` or `rounded-sm`), clean tabular metadata divisions.
- Buttons: Sharp rectangular buttons (`rounded-sm`), solid deep sage fill for primary actions, discrete outline for secondary.
- Containers: Crisp structured panels with subtle tonal shifts (`bg-surface` on `bg-background`), separated by clean hairline rules.
- Accent architecture: Deep sage applied to structural titles, active navigation states, and primary booking triggers.
- Sections: Generous padding (`py-16 md:py-24`), alternating between light surface and clean off-white canvas.
- Imagery: Contained architectural and practice interior photographs, neutral desaturated tones, no human faces.

## Do's and Don'ts
- DO maintain strict medical deontology and factual clarity throughout all copy.
- DO display the mandatory footer disclaimer on every page: « Cabinet fictif à visée de démonstration. »
- DON'T use wellness tropes (candles, pebbles, bamboo, flower graphics, spa palettes).
- DON'T create forms or free-text submission zones.
